Chalak Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026 · Contact: support@chalak.net

Chalak ("we", "us") provides fitness, nutrition, community, and marketplace features.

1. Who can use Chalak

Chalak is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support@chalak.net and we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: name, email, authentication identifiers (including Google Sign-In if you use it), role/plan.
  • Profile & fitness: workouts, routines, progress, body measurements you choose to enter, nutrition logs, goals.
  • Optional device permissions: camera / photos (used for profile avatars and food photo scanning; when you scan a meal, the photo is transmitted to Google Gemini for visual food identification and portion estimation, and is not retained or stored by Chalak), notifications (reminders), Activity Recognition / Health Connect steps and distance (optional; read and processed strictly on-device for activity tracking, never uploaded to our servers or shared).
  • Community & marketplace: posts, comments, reports, ads you create or save.
  • Technical: app version, crash diagnostics if enabled, and basic device/network info needed to run the service.
  • Payments: purchase tokens processed by Google Play; we verify entitlements on our servers. We do not store full payment card numbers.

3. How we use information

To provide workouts and nutrition tools, sync your data, moderate community safety, process subscriptions, send optional reminders, improve reliability, and respond to support requests.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that host our backend, Google (Sign-In, Play Billing, optional Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Google Gemini as a service provider for ephemeral meal scan photo processing), and only as required by law or to protect users from abuse. Health Connect data is processed strictly on your device and is not shared with any third party.

5. User-generated content

Posts and comments you publish may be visible to other users. You can report content. Staff may hide or remove content that violates these policies.

6. Retention & your choices

You can export or delete your account from Profile settings. For free accounts, progress measurement records (specifically body measurements, weekly summaries, and monthly summaries) auto-expire and are permanently deleted after 60 days. Other account, workout, nutrition, and profile records are retained until you delete them or your account is closed. After deletion we remove or anonymize personal data except where we must retain limited records for security, fraud, or legal reasons.

7. Security

We use industry-standard transport security (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@chalak.net

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date in the app and on this page.
